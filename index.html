About

What we do

Occam Inventions provides the engineering behind hardware products, working across mechanical design, electrical engineering, and controls. We work end to end: concept development, prototyping, design for manufacture, and the testing and documentation that regulated products require.

Engineering

Mechanical and electrical design, first prototype through production-intent hardware.

Controls & Systems

Closed-loop control and embedded systems that turn a concept into a working device.

Regulated Development

Design and documentation practices built for products that need to clear a regulatory pathway.

Systems Integration

Bringing mechanical, electrical, and software together into one coherent product.

Approach

How we work

A consistent process for taking hardware from a rough idea to something ready for the people who'll build and regulate it.

1

Concept

Define the problem, the constraints, and what a working version actually needs to do.

2

Design

Mechanical, electrical, and controls design worked together, not handed off in sequence.

3

Prototype & Test

Build, test, and iterate against real requirements, including the ones a regulator will check.

4

Manufacture Handoff

Design for manufacture and the documentation needed to move into production.
